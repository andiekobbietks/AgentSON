# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participation in AgentSON spaces should be respectful, constructive and focused on improving the project. We welcome people with different backgrounds, experience levels, disciplines and perspectives.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate, respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith while remaining willing to challenge technical claims with evidence.
- Explain disagreement without personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and help newcomers understand project context.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing and confidentiality.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation or personal attacks.
- Doxxing, threats or unwanted disclosure of private information.
- Publishing credentials, private session data or confidential material.
- Deliberate disruption, spam or repeated bad-faith conduct.
- Misrepresenting another person's work or contributions.

## Maintainer response

The maintainer may edit or remove content, decline contributions, restrict participation or block accounts where necessary to protect the community. Reports should be made privately through the repository owner's verified GitHub contact route.

This project is maintained on a best-effort basis. The maintainer will handle reports as fairly and promptly as practicable.
